# Natural auxiliary percussion

**Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)**, **Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner and contributors**. Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL ; official license statement: https://versilian-studios.com/vcsl/ . Licensed **CC0 1.0 Universal**, complete legal text supplied in `LICENSE`.

Real small/large shaker, two tambourines, six clap takes and four solo-clap velocities. Small shaker variants retain separate up/down strokes and original round-robin identifiers; they are different physical performances, not synthesized noise or pitch-shifted hi-hats. Bright alternatives are separate source recordings/instruments and are not claimed as extra velocity layers.

Changes: original channel layout retained; converted to 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM with lossless FLAC and WAV alternatives. Full recorded attacks and tails retained. The optional solo-clap recordings have their original 26–48 ms leading pad removed, retaining 3 ms before a -54 dB relative onset; all other recordings retain their original start. No per-sample normalization; one shared linear playback gain per articulation retains original take/velocity differences. Source/output hashes and exact transforms are in `provenance.json`.

This is a compact real percussion set, not a deep studio velocity/RR library; most shaker/tambourine articulations have two takes and one recorded dynamic.

Fixed source commit: `c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e`.

Every source file is verified against the Git blob SHA-1 from the fixed source tree. Source and distributed-file SHA-256 hashes are in `provenance.json`.
